Probe returns first samples from the far side of the Moon

Staff writer · 31 Oct 2026 · Source: Wikinews (CC BY)

lead image 16:9

Mission controllers confirmed on Friday that the return capsule landed safely and that all sample containers are intact. Engineers said the first inspection found no damage to the seals.

Scientists expect the material to answer long-standing questions about the age and composition of the lunar crust. Initial analysis will begin next week.
